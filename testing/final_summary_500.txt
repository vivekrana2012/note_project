**Unified Summary**

The Indian electric two-wheeler market has seen significant growth and challenges in recent months. The sector has been impacted by China's choking imports of critical supplies and shifts in government policy, particularly the PLI (Production-Linked Incentives) scheme.

Four companies, Ola Electric, Aether, Bajaj, and TVS, are being analyzed to understand their performance and outlook. Key statistics include:

* India ranks 2nd in the world's largest maker for two-wheelers.
* The Indian electric two-wheeler EV market grew from a hardly existing industry in 2020 to becoming the world's second-largest in terms of penetration by 2025, with an estimated 8% share.
* Aether sold approximately 65,600 units this quarter, up 67% year-on-year, with a sales worth of ₹941 crore and market share increasing from 12.1% to 17.4%.
* Ola Electric sold 52,666 units, with positive EBITDA for the first time due to extensive cost cutting.
* Bajaj TBS Motor's record quarterly opening revenue was ₹11,095 crore, with a profit before tax of ₹1266 crore and sales of approximately 15 lakh vehicles across categories in the last quarter.

The PLI scheme has boosted margins for scooter manufacturers like TVS and Ola, but also created challenges with charging infrastructure and after-sales service. Despite this, EV sales have hit an all-time high of 1.44 lakh units in October, driven by lower running costs compared to ICE vehicles.

QCOs (Quotas and Certification Orders) have been found to be ineffective in powering exports from India, despite initial growth in the first year. The Centre for the Study of Economic Policy (CSEP) concludes that QCOs do not lead to sustainable gains in exports.

Recently, the government has reevaluated QCOs, recommending scrapping or suspending over 200 orders, mostly on intermediate inputs. Reliance Industries has started investigating reliance communications for the period FY 2009 to 2024, adding to an ongoing ED probe into a legitimate diversion of Rs. 13,600 crore and $569 million in Yes Bank loans.
